# Billing and payment — demo policy
This is fictional billing guidance for a portfolio app.
Accepted payment methods are Visa, Mastercard and eligible debit cards in supported markets.
After an order is paid, a receipt is emailed to the registered email address. Download an invoice from Account > Orders > Invoice.
If your card was charged twice, check whether one transaction is pending; pending authorization holds may disappear automatically.
For confirmed duplicate charges, contact billing support with the order reference, not full card details.
Full card numbers and security codes must never be sent to support chat.
