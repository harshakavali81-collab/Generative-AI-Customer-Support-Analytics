# Account access — demo help center
This is sample documentation and does not grant access to any real account.
If you forget your password, open the Sign in page and choose Forgot password. Follow the password-reset email link.
Password reset links expire after 30 minutes. Never share your password or one-time sign-in code with a support agent.
For account deletion, contact the privacy team using the account settings form. A verified request is processed within 30 days, subject to legal retention obligations.
Two-factor authentication is available in Account > Security. Recovery codes should be stored in a safe location.
