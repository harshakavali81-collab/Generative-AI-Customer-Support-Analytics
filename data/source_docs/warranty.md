# Product warranty — demo terms
Fictional support knowledge base article; real manufacturers may differ.
Eligible devices include a 12-month limited warranty from the delivery date for manufacturing defects.
Accidental damage, liquid damage and unauthorized repairs are excluded from this limited warranty.
Customers can start a warranty claim in Account > Orders > Get support. A support agent may request a description and photo of the defect.
After verifying eligibility, support may offer repair or replacement. Warranty coverage is subject to local consumer law.
