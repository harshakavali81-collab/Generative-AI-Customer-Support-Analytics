# Shipping and order tracking — demo policy
This document describes a fictional store, not a real merchant.
Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 6 business days after dispatch. Express shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch.
The order confirmation email includes an order reference. Once dispatched, the tracking link is available in the Orders page.
If the tracking link has not updated for 48 hours after dispatch, contact demo support with the order reference.
Shipping estimates do not guarantee a delivery date. Weather and regional carrier disruptions may cause delays.
